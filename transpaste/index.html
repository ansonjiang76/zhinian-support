01

Translate and paste

翻译并直接粘贴

Press Option–Command–V in almost any text field. TransPaste handles the rest.

在常用输入框中按下 Option–Command–V，其余步骤交给 TransPaste。

⌥⌘V
02

Choose languages quickly

快速选择目标语言

Switch your default from the menu bar, or pick a language just for the next paste.

通过菜单栏切换默认语言，也可以只为下一次粘贴临时选择语言。

03

Paste again, instantly

重复粘贴，更快一步

The latest translation is reused for fast repeated pasting, while your original clipboard is preserved.

自动复用最近译文，连续粘贴无需重新翻译，同时保留原始剪贴板。

04

Private by design

隐私从设计开始

Text is translated locally using Apple’s Translation framework. TransPaste has no account system or translation history.

文字通过 Apple Translation 框架在本地翻译。TransPaste 没有账号系统，也不保存翻译历史。
