Frequently asked questions

常见问题

Does TransPaste need Accessibility permission?+

The App Store version uses macOS Services and does not require Accessibility or PostEvent permission. You explicitly invoke translation from Services or a shortcut. Some apps do not expose text Services; in version 2.0, copy the text and choose Translate Clipboard instead.

Why is the first translation slightly slower?+

The first request loads the on-device language model. TransPaste warms up your current language pair in the background after launch to reduce the delay.

What happens to my original clipboard?+

Translate-and-insert returns its result through the macOS Services pasteboard and preserves your original clipboard. Reading translation in version 2.0 changes the clipboard only when you click Copy Translation.

Can I paste the same translation more than once?+

Yes. The latest result is cached in memory for fast repeated pasting. Copying new text or changing the target language naturally replaces it.

Does TransPaste upload my text?+

No. Translation is performed on your Mac using Apple’s Translation framework. TransPaste does not operate a server for your clipboard content.

TransPaste 需要辅助功能权限吗？+

App Store 版通过 macOS 系统服务工作，无需辅助功能或 PostEvent 权限。翻译由你通过服务或快捷键主动触发。部分应用不提供文字服务；2.0 可先复制文字，再选择“翻译剪贴板”。

为什么第一次翻译稍慢？+

第一次请求需要加载本地语言模型。应用启动后会在后台预热当前语言组合，以减少首次使用的等待。

原来的剪贴板内容会怎样？+

“翻译并插入”通过 macOS 服务剪贴板返回译文，保留原始剪贴板。2.0 的阅读翻译仅在你点击“复制译文”时改变剪贴板。

同一段译文可以多次粘贴吗？+

可以。最近一次译文会保存在内存中供快速重复粘贴；复制新文字或切换目标语言后会自然替换。

TransPaste 会上传我的文字吗？+

不会。翻译通过 Apple Translation 框架在 Mac 本地处理，TransPaste 不设服务器接收剪贴板内容。

Privacy policy

隐私政策

Updated September 23, 2026

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 23 日

Overview

TransPaste is developed by Hainan Honglong Zhiyuan Technology Co., LTD. We designed the app to perform its core function without creating an account or sending clipboard text to our servers.

Clipboard data

TransPaste reads clipboard text only when you request a clipboard translation or prepare a language pair from copied text. Selected text in version 2.0 is supplied by macOS Services after your explicit action. Translate-and-insert returns text via Services; reading translation writes to your clipboard only when you choose Copy Translation.

Translation processing

Translation is performed on your device through Apple’s Translation framework. TransPaste does not operate a remote translation server and does not receive the source or translated text.

Data collection and retention

We do not collect personal information, clipboard content, translation history, keyboard input, screen content, analytics, advertising identifiers, or usage profiles through TransPaste. The most recent translation may remain temporarily in app memory to support repeated pasting and is cleared when the app exits or a new result replaces it.

System permission

The App Store version uses macOS Services and does not request Accessibility, screen recording, or PostEvent permission. It does not automatically monitor selections or typing.

Purchases in version 2.0

Apple processes purchases through the App Store. The app reads verified purchase identifiers, dates, expiry and revocation information to determine access. We do not receive payment card details and do not operate a separate account or entitlement server. Translation text is not part of the purchase process.

Children’s privacy

TransPaste does not knowingly collect information from children or from any other user.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published on this page.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email anson@healthverse.top.
